## Product Rules

- Recommended texture base minimum is 0.10mm, UI/config step is 0.01mm.
- Effective minimum is `max(0.10mm, 2 * classificationResolutionMm)`.
- Width can reach the dynamic all-texture threshold, where texture=model and fill=0.
- White, varnish, and RGB/custom use current W/V/RGB semantics.
- C/M/Y/K are MaterialProcessProfile roles resolved into RGBWSV, not new TIFF channels.

- Global failures never fall back silently to legacy.
- Clean models can advance functional development but cannot replace the three required model families.
